What it's for

JAS gives people one simple place to write things down, plan, and stay organized. Instead of juggling notes apps, spreadsheets, and chat threads, you see the whole plan on one board and move pieces around as it changes.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information JAS ("we") collects, how we use it, and your rights, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, email, and what you said you want to plan first.
  • Board content: titles, notes, shapes, labels, connectors, and images you add.
  • Cookie and display preferences.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To save and show your boards.
  • To improve JAS and its templates (only if you allow analytics cookies).

3. Where your data is stored

In this demo version, all data, including images on your boards, stays in your own browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to a server, and boards do not sync between devices. Clearing your browser data deletes your account and boards.

Your account (name, email, and a securely hashed password) and your boards, including images, are stored with our database provider, Supabase, so you can sign in on any device and share boards. Your password is never stored in readable form.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Boards are private to the browser they were made in.

We do not sell your personal information. A board is visible only to you and the people you add to it. People on the same board can see each other's name and email.

5. Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your data at any time. You can delete a board from your Boards page and withdraw cookie consent through Cookie settings.

6. Minors

Users below 18 should use JAS with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.

7. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@jas-planner.example

Using the board

Double-click empty space to add a sticky note. Double-click any note, text, or shape to edit it. Drag the green dot on a selected item onto another item to connect them.
